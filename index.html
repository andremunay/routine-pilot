Current sharing reality

Local files today, publishable links later.

Routine Pilot does not yet publish routines by link or QR code. In this beta, routines move through local export and import. Built-in-only routines can export as JSON; routines with ready custom sounds can export as Routine Pilot archives.

Privacy default

This page contains no analytics or tracking scripts. Routine Pilot keeps authoring and playback local-first. Optional Share Diagnostics in the app can send sanitized crash reports and coarse beta usage events only after explicit opt-in in Settings. It avoids routine names, cue text, tags, custom sound names, voice IDs, contacts, health data, location, and free text.
