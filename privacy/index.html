Privacy

Routine Pilot privacy

Routine Pilot is local-first. No account is required to author, play, export, or import routines.

Last updated

June 25, 2026

Routine names, cue text, tags, custom sounds, voice choices, and routine files stay on your device unless you choose to export, import, email, or share them through operating system surfaces.

Routine Pilot does not require accounts, ads, subscriptions, contacts, location, camera, microphone, wearable, Apple Health, or Health Connect access.

Local data

Routines and sounds

Saved routines, settings, and imported sounds are stored locally on the device.

Feedback

Tester notes

Feedback you send through the form, email, TestFlight, or Play testing is received by the developer.

Diagnostics

Opt-in only

Optional beta diagnostics, when configured, require explicit opt-in in Settings.

Contact

Privacy or support questions can be sent to andremunay@gmail.com.
